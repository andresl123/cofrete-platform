# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Autonomous Brazilian truck drivers and owner-operators, starting with a single-driver, one-truck MVP. Users are often deciding under time pressure whether a freight offer is worth accepting and need clear money separation before they commit to the trip.

## Product Purpose

Cofrete is a financial health and compliance assistant that helps drivers decide whether a freight is profitable and how much money is safe to use personally after direct costs, pass-through cash flow, reserves, taxes, documents, insurance, emergency needs, and future truck replacement.

## Brand Personality

Practical, conservative, and driver-friendly. The product should feel like a calm operating tool for work decisions, not a bank, government portal, tax filing product, or route-optimization app.

## Anti-references

Avoid consumer-finance gloss, official-government styling, gamified earnings celebration, generic SaaS dashboards, and any wording that implies guaranteed income, legal compliance, tax advice, accounting advice, insurance advice, or official record updates.

## Design Principles

- Put the accept, reject, or renegotiate freight decision first.
- Separate gross freight, pass-through cash flow, direct costs, reserves, profit, and safe personal withdrawal visibly.
- Use Portuguese-Brazil copy that is direct, conservative, and easy to scan in a cab or loading yard.
- Treat official and external data as advisory unless source metadata proves otherwise.
- Keep MVP flows focused on driver and truck setup, freight entry, profitability, reserves, and compliance risk.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use accessible contrast, clear touch targets, readable type, and copy that does not require accounting or compliance expertise. Avoid relying on color alone for risk or recommendation states.
