Cofrete Platform

Know what freight money is actually safe to use.

Cofrete is a Brazil-focused financial health and compliance assistant for autonomous truck drivers, MEI Caminhoneiro drivers, TAC operators, and later small fleet owners. It turns a freight offer into a practical answer: accept, renegotiate, or reject.

The simple idea

Many freight decisions look profitable when only gross freight is shown. Cofrete subtracts the real costs and future obligations first, then shows the amount the driver can safely use.

Money

Safe withdrawal, not vanity revenue

Gross freight is split into direct trip costs, pass-through toll items, required reserves, taxes, debt, driver salary, and profit.

Trust

Every result must be explainable

Calculations keep input and output snapshots so a driver, support user, or auditor can see how a number was produced.

Brazil

Localized to trucking realities

The MVP includes MEI Caminhoneiro support, ANP diesel data, RNTRC, CIOT, freight-floor checks, Vale-Pedagio, insurance, and IPVA/licensing reminders.

How a freight decision works

The driver enters a new freight or trip. Cofrete estimates real economics, separates pass-through money, checks compliance risks, and returns a decision-ready result.

1

Enter the offer

Origin, destination, total km, loaded km, freight value, payment timing, truck, customer, and route assumptions.

2

Estimate trip costs

Fuel, ARLA, tolls, meals, lodging, reimbursements, and direct expenses are calculated or recorded.

3

Reserve future money

Maintenance, tires, insurance, documents, taxes, truck replacement, and emergency reserves are allocated.

4

Check Brazil risks

CIOT, RNTRC, freight-floor context, Vale-Pedagio, insurance, waiting time, and receivables are surfaced as advisory checks.

5

Decide

The driver sees profit, safe withdrawal, confidence, and a practical recommendation to accept, renegotiate, or reject.

Main product modules

The planned MVP is mobile-first for drivers, with web reserved for admin, demo, support, and backoffice workflows unless a future issue expands it.

Onboarding

Driver and truck profile

Stores driver identity, truck details, tax profile, state, RNTRC metadata, insurance, financing, and income goals.

Calculator

Freight profitability

Computes gross freight, direct costs, reserves, profit, margin, safe withdrawal, and financial health status.

Wallet

Reserve buckets

Uses virtual envelopes for fuel cash flow, maintenance, tires, insurance, taxes, documents, truck replacement, emergency, salary, and profit.

Receivables

Payment tracking

Tracks advances, balances, Pix, boleto or transfer, Vale-Pedagio, reimbursements, partial payments, and late customer payments.

Maintenance

Truck cost intelligence

Builds toward cost-per-km, service history, tire lifecycle, downtime, and future preventive maintenance reminders.

Compliance

Advisory compliance center

Organizes RNTRC, CIOT, MEI, IPVA/licensing, insurance, CNH, document expiration, and official action links without pretending to be an official channel.

System shape

Cofrete is planned as a multi-service platform. Clients call the Core API. Heavy finance work and external data imports happen asynchronously through workers.

Mobile app Driver workflows, onboarding, calculator, wallet, compliance center.
Web app Admin, support, demo visibility, and operational dashboards.
Core API Authentication, product APIs, validation, and primary business data.
PostgreSQL Drivers, trips, expenses, reserves, documents, receivables, snapshots.
RabbitMQ events Trip recalculation requests, reserve allocation requests, and import completion events.
Finance worker Deterministic profitability, reserve, safe-withdrawal, and health score calculations.
Data importer worker ANP diesel prices, toll data, future ANTT freight-floor imports, and freshness status.

Important boundaries

The product is advisory software. It helps organize decisions, but it does not replace official systems, government portals, accountants, insurers, or legal guidance.

Not official

No government account proxy

Cofrete login is separate from gov.br, ANTT, Receita Federal, ANP, SUSEP, insurer, and state systems.

Pass-through

Tolls are not profit

Vale-Pedagio and toll reimbursements are tracked separately from profit and safe personal withdrawal.

Official links

Compliance copy points back to sources

When decisions affect tax, insurance, RNTRC, CIOT, freight floor, IPVA, or licensing, the product should link to official sources.
