ROU-220 / COF-016 Compliance Center Data Model And Wording

Manually maintained derived summary. Canonical source of truth remains the Markdown docs and service code.

What Changed

  • Core API now exposes the contracted compliance endpoints for profile, RNTRC metadata, public-status guidance, insurance policies, compliance score, calendar reminders, and document metadata.
  • Compliance persistence now covers driver RNTRC metadata, insurance policies, compliance documents, generated advisory alerts, score snapshots, and calendar items.
  • RNTRC and insurance responses include advisory wording that separates Cofrete app metadata from official ANTT, RNTRC Digital, gov.br, SUSEP, insurer, legal, tax, and accounting channels.
  • Expiration logic generates warning or critical alerts for insurance policies and documents that are expiring soon or already expired.

Why It Changed

ROU-220 prepares mobile compliance screens by giving them stable backend data for RNTRC status, insurance metadata, document reminders, compliance score components, and official-channel caveats.

Affected Areas

  • core-api/src/main/java/com/cofrete/coreapi/compliance: compliance controller, service, entities, repositories, request/response contracts, wording, and validation.
  • core-api/src/main/resources/db/migration/V7__compliance_center_model.sql: document, alert, score snapshot, and calendar persistence.
  • core-api/src/main/java/com/cofrete/coreapi/profile/ProfileLookupService.java: public driver/truck ownership lookup for compliance APIs.
  • core-api/src/test/java/com/cofrete/coreapi/compliance/ComplianceEndpointTests.java: endpoint coverage for metadata updates, advisory wording, scores, calendars, and expiration alerts.
  • CONTEXT.md: glossary entries for Compliance Profile, Compliance Alert, and Compliance Calendar.
  • docs/compliance/official-source-register.md: added RNTRC Digital source entry and ROU-220 source-check date.
