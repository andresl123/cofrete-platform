ROU-221 - COF-017 Implement receivables and customer payment tracking

What changed

Core API now has customer profiles, receivables, payment records, and receivable status history for freight customer cash-flow planning.

  • Added customer creation at POST /api/customers.
  • Added customer profitability at GET /api/customers/{customerId}/profitability.
  • Added receivable creation, overdue listing, and payment recording at the contracted receivable paths.
  • Added a Flyway migration for customers, receivables, receivable payments, and receivable status changes.
  • Added MockMvc coverage for lifecycle, overdue filtering, customer profitability inputs, and validation errors.

Why it changed

Financial health and future dashboards need late-payment risk, expected cash flow, and customer payment behavior. Cofrete still treats this as advisory planning data, not bank integration or guaranteed payment.
