# Glossary PT-BR

| Term | Meaning In Cofrete |
|---|---|
| ANP | Agencia Nacional do Petroleo, Gas Natural e Biocombustiveis; source for fuel price datasets. |
| ANTT | Agencia Nacional de Transportes Terrestres; regulator for road cargo transport topics used by Cofrete. |
| CIOT | Codigo Identificador da Operacao de Transporte; tracked as freight regularity metadata. |
| Cofrete | Product name combining "cofre" and "frete". |
| Frete bruto | Gross freight value before direct costs and reserves. |
| Lucro | Business result after costs and required reserves; not the same as cash received. |
| MEI Caminhoneiro | Brazilian MEI category for autonomous truck drivers, tracked as a tax profile mode. |
| RNTRC | Registro Nacional de Transportadores Rodoviarios de Cargas. |
| Saque seguro | Amount the driver can take personally after costs and reserves. |
| Vale-Pedagio | Toll payment advanced by the contractor; pass-through money, not profit. |
| Reserva | Bucketed amount kept for future or recurring obligations. |
| RCTR-C / RC-DC / RC-V | Transport liability insurance categories tracked as compliance and finance metadata. |
